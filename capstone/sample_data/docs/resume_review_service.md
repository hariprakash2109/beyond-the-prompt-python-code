---
title: Resume Review Service
access: public
---
Career Services offers free resume reviews to all students and recent graduates. Each appointment lasts 30 minutes and is held with a trained career advisor. Appointments can be booked online and must be booked at least 2 days in advance. Please bring a printed copy of your resume and, if you are applying for a specific role, the job description.

Walk-in reviews are available on Tuesday afternoons from 1 pm to 4 pm. Walk-ins are limited to 15 minutes and are served in order of arrival. Advisors will comment on layout, clarity, measurable achievements and keyword alignment with the job description. Advisors do not rewrite resumes for students; they give feedback and examples.

Expect written feedback by email within one business day after your appointment.
