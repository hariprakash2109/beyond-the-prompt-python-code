---
title: Employer Partner Contracts (Confidential)
access: staff
---
CONFIDENTIAL - staff only. Partner employer contracts are renewed each September. The standard placement fee agreed with partner employers is 8 percent of the first-year salary for graduate hires, and renewals above a threshold of ten hires per year receive a reduced rate. Contract negotiations are handled by the Director of Employer Relations. Do not share fee rates or renewal dates with students or with other employers.
