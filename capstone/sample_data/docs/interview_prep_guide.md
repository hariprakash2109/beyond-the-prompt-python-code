---
title: Interview Preparation Guide
access: student
---
Career Services runs mock interviews every Thursday. Sessions last 45 minutes and include written feedback. Students are encouraged to practice answering behavioral questions with the STAR method: describe the Situation, the Task, the Action you took, and the Result.

Before an interview, research the employer, re-read the job description, and prepare at least three questions to ask the interviewer. For video interviews, test your camera, microphone and internet connection in advance and choose a quiet room with a plain background. After the interview, send a short thank-you email within 24 hours.
