---
title: Laptop Loan Program
access: student
---
Enrolled students can borrow a laptop from the Career Services desk for up to 14 days. The loan can be renewed once for another 14 days if no other student is waiting. A valid student ID is required at pickup. No deposit is charged.

Laptops are intended for job applications, online interviews and coding assessments. Students must not install unlicensed software. Any damage or loss must be reported within 24 hours. Late returns incur a fee of 5 dollars per day, up to a maximum of 50 dollars. Students with unpaid late fees cannot borrow another laptop until the balance is cleared.
