---
title: Counselor Caseload Guidelines (Internal)
access: staff
---
INTERNAL - staff only. Each career counselor manages a caseload of no more than 120 students. Counselors must record every advising session in the case system within 48 hours. Students who miss two consecutive appointments receive a follow-up email, and a third missed appointment is referred to the Dean of Students office. Escalate any concern about a student's wellbeing to the Dean of Students office on the same day.
