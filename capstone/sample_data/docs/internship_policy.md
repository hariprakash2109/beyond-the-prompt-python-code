Riverside University internships can earn academic credit when they meet three conditions. First, the placement must last at least 8 weeks and no more than 24 weeks. Second, the student must have a faculty supervisor who agrees to review a short progress report at the midpoint of the placement. Third, the student must submit the internship approval form to Career Services at least 10 business days before the start date.

Students may earn up to 6 credits for a single internship. Unpaid internships additionally require a signed learning agreement that describes the skills the student expects to develop. International students should speak with the international office before accepting any placement, because work authorization rules may apply.

Internships that begin before approval cannot be credited retroactively.
